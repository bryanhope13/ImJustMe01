Life is a gift. Don't waste it on petty differences.

I'm Just Me started in Indianapolis with a simple goal: to help stop racism, profiling and stereotyping. Every piece carries a message that accepting each other for our differences makes life more fulfilling.

We make shirts, polos, hats and casual athletic wear so you can say "I'm just me" wherever you feel most comfortable, with pride in who you are regardless of race, ethnicity, sex or background.

    A letter from the founder

    Kyle Hope started I'm Just Me to ask a question he believes everyone should sit with. This is the letter he wrote to every customer.

    Hello World,

    I just wanted to take the time and thank you personally for your support. Here at I'm Just Me, our primary goal is to make people fully understand that life is truly a gift, and shouldn't be wasted on the petty differences in the world.

    We believe that we the people of the world can make a difference in how we look and act around each other. Actions speak louder than words, and my words are:

    "Does it really matter what color a person is or where they're from?"

    Dislikes and likes are always going to be a part of our lives, and that's what makes us so unique from each other. But to hate someone because of their race, sex, religion or background is the most foolish reason to dislike a person.

    Just being a person and surviving in today's world is hard enough. So don't dislike someone until you start living the perfect lifestyle and have all the answers about right and wrong.

    I'm just me, and that's all I can be in this world: to survive and treat everyone fairly, with kindness, respect and honor. There are still people in the world who reject peace and tranquility in our human society, but here at I'm Just Me we believe everyone can change for the world's best interest.

    So spread our message to everyone: stop racism, profiling and stereotypes, before our children have to clean up our mistakes.

    Thank you so very much for your support, Kyle Hope CEO and Founder, I'm Just Me LLC
